This document collects amendments to the rules contained within Ravening Hordes and associated Arcane Journals, and
presents our responses to players’ frequently asked questions. As this document is revised regularly, it has a version number;
where a version number has a letter, e.g. 1.1a, this means it has had a local amendment, only in one language, to clarify a
translation issue. When this document is revised in full, the version number will be incremented.
