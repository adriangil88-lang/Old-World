CITED SPECIAL RULES (paraphrased/partially quoted by fetch tool)
================================================================================
/special-rules/rallying-cry (Rulebook p. 175): In the Command sub-phase of its turn, if not engaged in combat, the character may nominate a single fleeing friendly unit within its Command range; "The nominated unit immediately makes a Rally test." If failed, the unit may attempt to rally again as normal in the Rally sub-phase.
/special-rules/veteran (p. 180): "Veteran warriors have seen and done it all, and it takes a lot to unsettle them." "If the majority of the models in a unit have this special rule, the unit may re-roll any failed Leadership test." "Note that a Break test is not a Leadership test."
/special-rules/warband: Keen for the fray but easily dismayed. Unless fleeing, adds a positive Leadership modifier equal to its current Rank Bonus, capped at Ld10. Not usable for Restraint tests; if Impetuous, not usable when checking whether it must declare a charge or may act normally. If most models have the rule, the unit may re-roll its Charge roll. A character's Leadership is not modified unless it has the rule. A Warband may use the highest of: its own modified Ld, the modified Ld of a Warband character, or the unmodified Ld of a non-Warband character.
/special-rules/drilled (p. 167): A non-fleeing Drilled unit may perform a free "redress the ranks" manoeuvre just before it moves, then moves as usual. May march within 8" of an enemy without a Leadership test. Characters joining are treated as Drilled.
/special-rules/phalanx: (page returned the Close Order rule text in error; Phalanx wording NOT obtained.) Returned text, apparently for Close Order: a Close Order unit in which the majority of models have the rule counts as being in Combat Order, not Marching Column, even if deeper than wide, as long as enough models per rank for a rank bonus; while deeper than wide it can claim rank bonus and charge but "cannot march at triple its Movement characteristic"; if charged by a unit with First Charge "it does not become Disrupted"; to adopt Marching Column it must be deeper than wide and have fewer models per rank than needed for a rank bonus. [UNVERIFIED which rule this belongs to]
/special-rules/motley-crew: Units may mix models equipped differently or of different types. Different weapons: a fighting rank may include differing weapons; roll separate dice per model, state which model's attacks are made and where directed; attacks in each model's own Initiative order. Different armour: in combat use the armour value shared by most models in the fighting rank; against shooting, the value shared by most models in the unit. Casualties: vs shooting spread as evenly as possible; in combat from the majority models in the fighting rank; controlling player chooses which rear-rank models fill gaps.
/special-rules/levies (p. 172): conscripted soldiers; Levy models cannot benefit from the General's Inspiring Presence or a Battle Standard's "Hold your Ground". Units without Levies need not take Panic tests when a friendly Levy unit Breaks and flees from combat.
/special-rules/untutored-arcanist (AJ Bretonnia p. 42): "Many Outcast Wizards are poorly schooled in the arcane arts." "Thus, when their spellcasting falters, great calamity can befall them." "When required to roll on the Miscast table, a Wizard with this special rule must roll an extra D6," then keeps only the lower result.
/special-rules/noble-disdain (AJ p. 43): a model with it whose troop type is Cavalry does not have to make a Panic test when (1) a friendly Infantry unit is destroyed within 6", or (2) a friendly Infantry unit flees through the model.
/special-rules/lance-formation (Forces of Fantasy p. 107): "Bretonnian knights make use of a powerful formation called the 'Lance'." "This formation enables them to charge deep into enemy lines, causing great devastation." "A unit consisting of models with this special rule may adopt a Lance formation." (Formation details not on this page.)
/special-rules/ambushers (Rulebook p. 166): may start in reserve instead of deploying. From round 2, in each of your Start of Turn sub-phases roll a D6 per Ambushers unit in reserve: 1-3 delayed until at least next turn; 4+ arrives as reinforcements in the Compulsory Moves sub-phase. Controlling player chooses any battlefield edge; cannot be placed within 8" of an enemy model. Any still in reserve at the start of round 5 arrive automatically.
/special-rules/tilean-stoicism (AJ p. 43): "Should a character with this special rule join a unit prior to making their first Break Test of the game" the unit gains Stubborn, only until the character's first Break Test; joining after that has no effect. If the character leaves a unit they joined before its first Break Test, the unit immediately loses Stubborn for any reason.

/magic-items/infamous-origins (only Renegade Lords [Prince/Captain] and Outcast Wizards of a Renegade Crowns list; one origin each):
 1. The Might of Miragliano, 25 pts: +1 Leadership; gains Stubborn and Tilean Stoicism.
 2. The Renegade Knight, 25 pts: +1 WS and +1 S; gains Noble Disdain and Veteran; may buy magic items from the Kingdom of Bretonnia lists (FoF and AJ Bretonnia).
 3. The Noble Outlaw, 20 pts: +1 BS and +1 Ld; gains Hold the Line; may buy magic items from the Empire lists (FoF and AJ Empire of Man). Weapon (one): handgun +6, repeater handgun +8, repeater pistol +8, shortbow +2, longbow +4. Special rule (one): Ambushers +10 or Scouts +10.
 4. The Wandering Diestro, 20 pts: +2 WS; gains Feint & Dodge; may carry an additional hand weapon +3; cannot carry weapons with Strike Last.
/special-rules/feint-and-dodge (AJ War of Settra's Fury p. 43): once per game, when an enemy model directs attacks against a model with this rule, you may force that enemy model to re-roll its successful To Hit rolls. (The Wandering Duelists page repeats it for Brigands.)
/special-rules/hold-the-line (FoF p. 48): the model and any unit it has joined automatically pass any Panic tests.
/special-rules/phalanx (AJ p. 37; the earlier "Close Order text" IS the Phalanx rule): in a Close Order unit where most models have Phalanx the unit counts as in Combat Order (not Marching Column) even if deeper than wide, as long as it has enough models per rank for rank bonus; deeper than wide it can claim rank bonus and make charge moves but cannot march at triple Movement; if charged by a unit with First Charge it is not Disrupted; to adopt Marching Column it must be deeper than wide with fewer models per rank than needed for rank bonus.
/special-rules/noble-disdain (AJ p. 43): a Cavalry model with it need not take Panic tests when a friendly Infantry unit is destroyed within 6" or flees through the model.
