                           Upon Shrouded Shores
I   n the early years of the Chainmaker’s invasion of the Empire’s northern coasts, the High Elves of the Shrouded Isle had
    withdrawn from the affairs of the Men of Westerland, the Sea Lord tending instead to the mysterious affairs of the Ulthuan.
Thus did Aislinn’s sleek vessels seemingly neglect the waters where the Sea of Claws meets the Manaansport Sea, giving the
Wolves of the Sea the impression that they could ply the waves unchallenged. In time, glory-hungry warlords began to wonder
if the isle had been abandoned. The first tribes to make landfall upon its shores would swiftly learn the error of their ways…
