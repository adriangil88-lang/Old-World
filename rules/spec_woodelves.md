## 0. Critical notes
1. **Army lists available**: Grand Army (FoF p.119) + two Armies of Infamy in AJ: **Orion's Wild Hunt** (AJ p.28-29) and **Host of Talsyn** (AJ p.32-33).
2. **Names that the brief guessed but do NOT exist** in either source: "Forest Spirit" (the rule is **Tree Spirit**), "Forest Stalker", "Blessings of Asuryan" (High Elf rule). The army deployment rule is **Woodland Ambush** (an extra wood). **Kindreds** exist only in the AJ (Alter / Noble Kindreds, sec. 3.3).
3. **Glade Lord/Captain do NOT have Elven Reflexes** (they have Strike First, not the mount). Shadowdancer: no Elven Reflexes (Strike First). Waystalker, Mages: Elven Reflexes. Tree spirits (Dryads, Tree Kin, Treemen, Treeman Ancient, Branchwraith): no Elven Reflexes.
4. **Lores**: Spellweaver/Spellsinger = Battle Magic, Elementalism, High Magic, Illusion (+ Lore of Athel Loren). Shadowdancer (if Wizard) = Battle Magic, Illusion (**no** Lore of Athel Loren). Treeman Ancient = Battle Magic, Elementalism (+ Lore of Athel Loren). Branchwraith = Battle Magic, Elementalism, Illusion (+ Lore of Athel Loren). Sisters of the Thorn = 1 spell from Battle Magic or Elementalism as Bound spell. **Lore of the Wilds** (AJ) only via Host of Talsyn "In Tune With Nature" or Heartwood Pendant.
5. **Unit sizes**: "5+" no max for most; Tree Kin **2+**; Warhawk Riders **3+**; characters, Treemen, Great Eagle **1**; Orion **3** (Orion + 2 Hounds); Beast Packs **1+ Beast Keepers** (each with 1-3 beasts or 1 Bear).
6. **Forest Spites**: not magic items; **each Spite once per army**; bought from a per-model Spite allowance (sec. 9).

| Characters | up to 50% | 0-1 **Glade Lord or Spellweaver** per 1,000 pts; 0-1 **Treeman Ancient** per 1,000 pts; unlimited: Glade Captains, Spellsingers, Shadowdancers, Waystalkers, Branchwraiths |

| Mercenaries | up to 20% | no units named [core] |
| Allies | up to 25%, single contingent | any Wood Elf Realms Army of Infamy list (Orion's Wild Hunt / Host of Talsyn); or Grand Army of Empire of Man (**Uneasy**), Kingdom of Bretonnia, High Elf Realms |

### 1.4 Scenario (AJ p.18-19, "The Prophet & the Hunter")
Historical recreation: attacker 2,500 pts Orion's Wild Hunt (General = Orion) vs defender 1,500 pts main force + 2,000 pts ambushing force Beastmen (General Kralmaw). Herdstone special feature (Arcane Monolith + Monument of Glory). Attacker takes first turn; 6 rounds. Roaming Warherds (ambush force gets Ambushers free); Fighting to Safety (after Kralmaw slain, attacker units touching a board edge are removed, not destroyed); Vengeful Wrath (attacker units Impetuous). Bonus VP: defender +250 if Orion slain; attacker +50 per unit that escaped. (OCR partly garbled; layout details on map not transcribed.)

## 2. Armour & weapons conventions
- Armour [core]: light 6+, heavy 5+, full plate 4+, none 7+; shield +1; barding +1; never better than 2+. **Armoured Hide (X)** improves AV by X [core p.166]. Wizards cannot wear armour and cast [core] (no Wood Elf exemption exists).

### 3.1 Character options
- **Glade Lord/Captain** (p.120): one of: additional hand weapon +3, great weapon +4, cavalry spear (if appropriately mounted) +2; shield +2; one enchanted arrow type: Arcane bodkins +6, Hagbane tips +6, Moonfire shot +3, Swiftshiver shards +6, Trueflight arrows +3; mount (sec. 3.2); Forest Spites up to 50; magic items: Lord 100, Captain 50. Captain may be BSB +25. Kindred (sec. 3.3, AJ).
- **Spellweaver/Spellsinger** (p.121): Lore = Battle Magic, Elementalism, High Magic, Illusion; **Talismanic Tattoos +15**; mount (sec. 3.2); Spites up to 50; Spellweaver Level 4 +30, magic items 100; Spellsinger Level 2 +30, magic items 50. Kindred (AJ).
- **Shadowdancer** (p.122): one of Spear of Loec (free) or Trickster's Blades (free); Spites up to 50; **Level 1 Wizard +35** (Battle Magic or Illusion); magic items 50. No mount.
- **Waystalker** (p.123): additional hand weapon +3; enchanted arrows (same costs as Lord); light armour +3; Spites up to 50; **Ambushers +10**; magic items 50. No mount.
- **Treeman Ancient** (p.124): Level 3 +30 or Level 4 +60; Spites up to **100**. **No magic item allowance printed** (NOT IN SOURCES).
- **Branchwraith** (p.125): one of additional hand weapon +3 or great weapon +4; Level 2 +30; Spites up to 50. **No magic item allowance printed.**
- **Warden of Talsyn** (AJ p.38): may replace Asrai spear with additional hand weapon +3 or great weapon +4; shield +2; magic items up to **75**. May be BSB in Host of Talsyn.
- **Araloth / Orion**: "must be fielded as presented" (no options).
- **The Arrow of Kurnous** (Lord/Captain): after deployment, before the roll-off for first turn, if the enemy General is within 36" of one or more models with this rule, one of them may fire it: the enemy General suffers **1 hit S3, no armour or Regeneration save** (Ward allowed). If fired, the opponent adds **+1** to their roll-off for first turn.
- **Troubadour of Loec** (Shadowdancer): when joining a unit of Wardancers, has **Dances of Loec** while with the unit.
- **Hawk-eyed Archer** (Waystalker): can target any enemy character in line of sight regardless of the usual rules for targeting Lone characters; may also target a specific model within its target unit (champion or character).
- **Courage Beyond Compare** (Warden): if joins Guardians of Talsyn, that unit gains Immune to Psychology (lost if he leaves).
- **Parry** (Warden, Guardians of Talsyn): when fighting with hand weapon + shield, or Asrai spear + shield, the unit improves its AV by 1.
- **Boldest of the Bold** (Araloth): ignores all negative modifiers to his Leadership.
- **Favour of the Goddess** (Araloth): **Ward 5+**.
- **Skaryn the Eye Thief** (Araloth): in his Command sub-phase roll D6: **1-2** nothing; **3+** nominate one enemy model within **18"**: it suffers **1 hit S4 AP-1**; if it suffers an unsaved wound, its **WS and I are reduced by D3** (min 1) for the rest of the game.
- **Cloak of Isha** (Orion): **Ward 5+**; in each of your Start of Turn sub-phases roll D6: on **5+** Orion recovers 1 lost Wound.
- **Horn of the Wild Hunt** (Orion): once per game, in his Command sub-phase: until end of turn, friendly units in Orion's Command range gain **Frenzy** (even if Frenzy was lost earlier).
- **Spectral Hounds**: Hounds of Orion can only be wounded by **Magical attacks**.

### 3.3 Wood Elf Kindreds (AJ p.42-43)
A character may belong to **either** an Alter Kindred **or** a Noble Kindred (not both); selected at list writing; benefits **do not apply to the mount**. Only **Wood Elf Nobles and Mages** (Glade Lord/Captain, Spellweaver/Spellsinger). No army list restriction printed (usable in any Wood Elf list (†)).

**Alter Kindreds** — units joined by a character with an Aspect **cannot use that character's Leadership**.
| Aspect | Pts | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect of the Hound | +20 | Character and any unit joined may **re-roll To Hit rolls of a natural 1** in the Combat phase |
| Aspect of the Bear | +15 | 'Infantry' or 'cavalry' only. **+1 S and +1 T** |
| Aspect of the Boar | +10 | **+1 AP** (improved by 1) to its weapon(s) in a turn it charged; gains **Impact Hits (1)** |
| Aspect of the Cat | +10 | Gains **Armour Bane (1)**; may **re-roll failed To Hit rolls during a challenge** |

## 13. Notes / ambiguities
1. **AJ OCR is badly garbled** (profiles, points, Orion unit size read as "7", Hound row, Beast Pack table, Kindreds, item points). All AJ rules values in this spec were taken from **rendered PDF page images** (printed p.28-29, 32-48), not from `aj_wood.txt`. Fluff pages (p.3-27) are mostly unreadable in OCR and contain no list rules except the scenario (p.18-19, partly garbled; summarised only).
2. **Orion**: printed "Unit Size: 3*", "Magic Resistance (2)" (no minus sign) and "Strikes First" (sic, = Strike First). Battlefield role/slot not stated beyond "may be included in any Wood Elf Realms army… any composition list"; treat as Character (counts toward Characters %) (†). Does not occupy a Glade Lord slot as far as printed. No magic items/Spites.
3. **Araloth** "is a Glade Lord": presumably counts against 0-1 Glade Lord (or Spellweaver) per 1,000 and has no Arrow of Kurnous/Glade Lord rules beyond those printed. Not stated whether he can take Kindreds (literal reading: no, "fielded as presented").
