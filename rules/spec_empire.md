# Spec Empire of Man (Warhammer: The Old World) - datos exactos

Fuente: `Forces_of_Fantasy.pdf`. **Citas = página FÍSICA del PDF** (la impresa es física-1). Perfiles verificados contra imagen de página en las tablas con columnas desplazadas (Steam Tank, War Altar, Griffons, Cañón/Mortero, Pegasus/Demigryph). Marcas: `(†)` = dato derivado de reglas del Core Rulebook (no impreso en el perfil); `[core]` = regla/arma definida en Core_Rulebook.pdf, no en FoF.

## 0. Alcance del libro
- Es la lista general **Grand Army Composition List** del Empire of Man (p.48), sin reglas de provincia/estado: los estados (Reikland, Talabecland, Nuln...) solo aparecen como trasfondo. **No hay Army Special Rules de ejército** ni arquetipos en este libro (no hay página de reglas de ejército). Las Armies of Infamy del Empire solo se mencionan (p.48 Allies) pero no están en este libro. No hay personajes con nombre.
- No existe unidad "Reiksguard": hay **Empire Knights** (Knightly Orders, p.65) e **Inner Circle Knights** (p.66). Halberdiers/Spearmen/Swordsmen son opciones de **State Troops** (p.58); Handgunners/Crossbowmen son **State Missile Troops** (p.59). "General (Captain/Count/Elector/Generalissimo)" = **General of the Empire** y **Captain of the Empire** (p.49); "Mage" = **Master Mage** / **Wizard Lord** (p.50).

Armadura [core p.221]: ligera 6+, pesada 5+, coraza completa 4+, sin armadura 7+; escudo mejora 1; montura con barding mejora 1; nunca mejor que 2+. Los Wizards no pueden llevar armadura/escudo. ## 7. Reglas especiales con texto exacto (particulares del libro, FoF)
**"Hold the Line!" (49)**: This character and any unit they have joined automatically passes any Panic tests they are required to make.
**Master of Battle (51)**: If this model joins a unit of Empire Knights, Inner Circle Knights or Demigryph Knights, that unit will gain the Immune to Psychology special rule. Should this model leave a unit it has joined for any reason, that unit loses this special rule.
**Suffer Not... (52)**: Each Witch Hunter must have one specialism, declared at deployment: The Witch: the Witch Hunter and any unit they have joined gains Hatred (enemy Wizards) and Magic Resistance (-2). The Revenant: Hatred (Undead models) and Flaming Attacks. The Mutant: Hatred (Warriors of Chaos & Beasts of Chaos) and Magic Resistance (-1). The Daemon: Hatred (Daemonic models) and Magical Attacks.
**Prayers of Sigmar (53)**: During the Command sub-phase of their turn, this character may attempt to invoke Sigmar's blessings by chanting a single Prayer from the list below and making a Leadership test (using their own Leadership). If passed, the Prayer takes immediate effect: *Hammer Of Sigmar*: Until your next Start of Turn sub-phase, this model, its mount and a single friendly unit within its Command range when chanted may re-roll any rolls To Hit or To Wound of a natural 1 made during the Combat phase. *Shield Of Faith*: Until your next Start of Turn sub-phase, this model and a single friendly unit within its Command range has a 5+ Ward save against any wounds suffered during the Shooting phase. *Soulfire*: A single enemy unit this model is engaged in combat with immediately suffers D6 Strength 3 hits, each with an AP of -2. These hits have the Flaming Attacks and Magical Attacks special rules. (Si hay varias unidades en rango, indicar cuál; los efectos no se acumulan sobre la misma unidad.)
**Prayers of Ulric (54)**: Mismo procedimiento (Command sub-phase, test de Ld propio). *Battle Howl*: Until your next Start of Turn sub-phase, when this model and a single friendly unit within its Command range makes a Charge roll, you may apply a +D3 modifier to the result. Only models whose troop type is 'infantry' or 'cavalry'. *Winter's Chill*: Until your next Start of Turn sub-phase, any enemy model that directs its attacks against this model, its mount or any unit it has joined during the Combat phase must re-roll any rolls To Hit of a natural 6. *Wrath Of Winter*: Until your next Start of Turn sub-phase, this model, its mount and any unit it has joined gains the Multiple Wounds (2) special rule.
**Clouds of Soot & Smoke (55)**: This model is considered to be behind full cover whilst it is within 3" of a friendly unit whose troop type is 'war machine'.
**Master of Ballistics (55)**: Unless this model is fleeing or engaged in combat, once per turn a friendly Empire war machine that is within its Command range can either use this model's Ballistic Skill characteristic, or re-roll a single Artillery dice.
**Fanatical Zeal (62)**: A unit with this special rule can be joined by a Lector of Sigmar or a Priest of Sigmar. However, these are the only characters that can join this unit. A Lector of Sigmar or Priest of Sigmar that joins a unit of Flagellants is considered to have the Unbreakable special rule for as long as they remain with the unit.
**Feel No Pain (62)**: A model with this special rule has: a 6+ Ward save against any wounds suffered that were caused by a non-magical enemy attack; a 5+ Ward save against any wounds suffered that were caused by a non-magical enemy attack with a Strength 5 or higher.
**Inner Circle (66)**: When engaged in combat, a model with this special rule (but not its mount) may re-roll any rolls To Hit of a natural 1.
**Holy Fervour (68)**: Unless this unit is fleeing, friendly units within 6" of it automatically pass any Fear tests they are required to make and can re-roll any failed Panic tests.
**Symbol of Might (68)**: The Command range of a Lector of Sigmar mounted on the War Altar is increased from 8" to 12". If this model is your General, it has a Command range of 18" as normal.
**Witch Bane (68)**: All Wizards within this model's Command range (friend or foe) suffer a -2 modifier to their Casting rolls.
**Grinding Wheels (69)**: Stomp Attacks made by a Steam Tank have an Armour Piercing characteristic of -2. Cannot be used against models whose troop type is 'behemoth'. In addition, and unlike other chariots, this model treats low linear obstacles as open terrain rather than as impassable terrain.
**Steam Power (69)**: A Steam Tank cannot march. Instead, you may make a 'Steam Power' roll and add the result to the model's Movement characteristic. To make a Steam Power roll, roll two D6 and discard the lowest result. The highest result is the result of the Steam Power roll. If both dice roll the same result, discard either.
**Temperamental (70)**: If a natural 1 is rolled on either of the dice when the Steam Tank makes a Steam Power roll or a Charge roll, choose: *Bang!* The Steam Tank loses a single Wound. *Phweee!* The Steam Tank halts immediately and cannot move again for the remainder of this turn. If a natural 1 is rolled on both dice, the Steam Tank loses a single Wound and halts immediately; it cannot move again for the remainder of this turn.
**Steam cannon notes (70)**: This weapon shoots like a cannon, using 'Cannon Fire'. If a 'Misfire' is rolled on the Artillery dice during step 2, this model loses a single Wound (instead of rolling on a Misfire table). **Steam gun notes**: No armour save is permitted against wounds caused by this weapon (Ward and Regeneration saves can be attempted as normal).
**Two Heads (71)**: An Imperial Griffon with Two Heads gains a +1 modifier to its Attacks characteristic.
**Helblaster (73)**: When shooting, roll three Artillery dice before making any rolls To Hit. The number of shots fired is equal to the total of all three Artillery dice: Misfire on one die: all shots suffer an additional -1 To Hit; Misfire on two dice: no shots are fired and a critical fault has occurred, roll on the Misfire table; Misfire on all three: fires 30 shots, but is destroyed and removed from play, all shots suffer an additional -1 To Hit. Then roll To Hit using the crew's Ballistic Skill.
**Helstorm (74)**: 1. Choose Target: no line of sight required; choose an enemy unit between min and max range and place D3 blast templates with the central hole over that unit. 2. Scatter: for each template roll an Artillery dice and a Scatter dice: 'Misfire' on Artillery = rocket fails (roll once on the Misfire table per Misfire after all shots are resolved); 'Hit!' on Scatter = move the template in the small arrow's direction a number of inches equal to the Artillery roll minus the crew's BS (min 0); an arrow = move the template the Artillery roll in that direction. 3. Damage: any model whose base lies under a template risks being hit.

### Armas y objetos del Empire (p.75-78)

## 9. Datos que faltan o son dudosos
1. Máximo de tamaño de unidad: no impreso salvo Demigryph Knights (2-12); límites de campaña/escala quedan a decisión del motor.
2. Mercenaries (20%): el libro no lista unidades.
3. Mortar sin "Special Rules" impreso (Great Cannon/Helblaster/Helstorm tienen Skirmishers); tratarlo como dato ausente.
4. Texto ambiguo de opciones de Empire/Inner Circle Knights ("debe tomar great weapons o lances; O puede cambiar escudos por great weapons gratis"); interpretación probable: lanza o great weapon (la alternativa sustituye al escudo).
5. Spell lists de los Lores (Battle Magic, etc.), tablas Misfire y reglas de Command range viven en el Core Rulebook, no en FoF.
6. Perfiles de monturas: BS/T/W "-" tal cual impreso; el jinete usa su propio T/W (las filas del caballero en caballería usan T/W del jinete).
