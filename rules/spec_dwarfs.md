# Spec Dwarfen Mountain Holds (Warhammer: The Old World) - datos exactos

Fuente: `Forces_of_Fantasy.pdf` (FoF), sección Dwarfen Mountain Holds = **PDF 10-40** (lista de ejército PDF 12-39, reglas de ejército PDF 40; PDF 41-45 son ilustraciones/fotos sin reglas). **Citas = página FÍSICA del PDF** (la impresa es física-1). Perfiles verificados contra imagen de página donde la extracción de texto partía columnas (Anvil of Doom PDF 15, Gyrobomber PDF 30). Marcas: `(†)` = dato derivado de reglas del Core Rulebook (no impreso en el perfil); `[core pN]` = regla/arma definida en Core_Rulebook.pdf (página física), no en FoF.

## 0. Alcance del libro
- Solo existe la **Grand Army Composition List** (PDF 12). **No hay Armies of Infamy de Dwarfs en este libro** (la lista de Allies las menciona, PDF 12, pero no están en FoF ni en ningún otro archivo disponible). No hay personajes con nombre.
- **Los Dwarfs no tienen Wizards ni magia de Lores**: no pueden comprar objetos mágicos del Core (PDF 33); usan **runic items** (sección 8). La única "magia" propia son los Bound spells del **Anvil of Doom** (Strike the Runes) y del **Master Rune of Calm**, y la capacidad de dispersar por **Rune Lore** (Runelord, Runesmith, Anvil).
- Nombres: "Lord/General" = **King** / **Thane** (Dwarf Lords, PDF 13); "Runesmith/Runelord" = **Dwarf Runesmiths** (PDF 17); "Slayer" personaje = **Daemon Slayer** / **Dragon Slayer** (Slayers of Legend, PDF 18); unidad de Slayers = **Troll Slayers** con upgrade a **Giant Slayers** (PDF 28). Quarrellers = ballesteros; Thunderers = arcabuceros (misma entrada, PDF 22).
- No hay caballería montada en animales: la "caballería" son **Gyrocopters** (monstrous cavalry) y **Gyrobombers** (monstrous creature). No hay monturas salvo **Shieldbearers** (opción de King/Thane).

## 2. Convenciones de objetos
Armadura [core p.221-222]: ligera 6+, pesada 5+, coraza completa (full plate) 4+, sin armadura 7+; escudo mejora 1; nunca mejor que 2+. Tamaño de unidad: el libro imprime "5+" (mínimo, sin máximo) en todas las infanterías; **únicos rangos impresos: Gyrocopters 1-6**; personajes, Gyrobomber y máquinas = 1. Todas las bases 25x25 salvo indicación.
- Reglas comunes a casi todo el ejército: **Hatred (Orcs & Goblins)**, **Magic Resistance (-1)** (o mejor), **Resolute**. Excepciones impresas: Gyrocopters y Gyrobombers **no** tienen ninguna de las tres; Slayers (personajes y unidad) tienen MR (-2).

## 7. Reglas especiales con texto exacto
### Reglas de ejército (PDF 40)
**Deathblow**: When a model with this special rule is reduced to zero Wounds by an enemy attack during the Combat phase, the unit that made the attack suffers a Strength 3 hit, with an AP of -1. Note that if this model is reduced to zero Wounds whilst engaged in a challenge, it is the model that made the attack that suffers this hit, rather than its unit.
**Dwarf Crafted**: Models with this special rule do not suffer the usual -1 To Hit modifier when making a Stand & Shoot charge reaction.
**Gromril Armour**: A model with this special rule may re-roll any roll of a natural 1 made when making an Armour Save roll.
**Gromril Weapons**: A hand weapon carried by a model with this special rule has an Armour Piercing characteristic of -1. Note that this special rule only applies to a single, ordinary hand weapon. If the model is using two hand weapons or any other sort of weapon, or if their hand weapon is inscribed with any Weapon runes, this special rule ceases to apply.
**Resolute**: Models with this special rule suffer a -1 modifier to the result of any Flee roll or Pursuit roll they make (to a minimum of 1).
**Rune Lore**: A model with this special rule may be nominated to attempt a Wizardly Dispel, as if it were a Wizard. For the purposes of Wizardly Dispel attempts: An Anvil of Doom counts as a level 3 Wizard; A Runelord counts as a Level 2 Wizard; A Runesmith counts as a Level 1 Wizard. (Dispel range [core p.111]: Nivel 1-2 = 18"; Nivel 3-4 = 24"; no en combate, huyendo ni fuera del campo.)

### Reglas de unidad (FoF)
**Ancestral Grudge (13)**: A model with this special rule has the Hatred (enemy characters) special rule, meaning it hates all characters in the opposing army. If this character joins a unit of Longbeards or Hammerers, that unit will also gain this special rule. Should this character leave a unit of Longbeards or Hammerers they have joined for any reason, that unit loses this special rule.
**Ancestral Shield (15)**: An Anvil of Doom and the Forgefather & Anvil Guard have a 5+ Ward save against any wounds suffered.
**Immovable Object (15)** (impreso en la página del Anvil pero **no** en su línea "Special Rules"): Once an Anvil of Doom has been placed on the battlefield during deployment it cannot be moved by its crew during the Remaining Moves sub-phase. Note that an Anvil of Doom can still pivot freely at any time during its turn (the better to face the enemy) and may make a follow up move as normal.
**Strike the Runes (16)**: An Anvil of Doom can cast the following Bound spells, with a Power Level of 3:
- *Rune of Oath & Steel* — Enchantment, CV **7+/11+**, Range 24". Con resultado 7+: la unidad amiga objetivo puede repetir las tiradas de salvación por armadura falladas. Con 11+: además mejora su armour value en 1 (máximo 2+). Dura hasta tu próximo Start of Turn sub-phase.
- *Rune of Hearth & Home* — Enchantment, CV **7+**, Range Self. "Until your next Start of Turn sub-phase, all friendly Dwarf units within 21" of the Anvil of Doom gain the Immune to Psychology special rule."
- *Rune of Haste & Urgency* — Conveyance, CV **10+**, Range 24". "If the target friendly unit is not fleeing and has already moved during this Movement phase, it may immediately move again."
- *Rune of Wrath & Ruin* — Magic Missile, CV **9+**, Range 27". "The target enemy unit suffers 2D6 Strength 4 hits, each with an AP of -2."
- Bound spells [core p.110]: Power Level se suma a la tirada de lanzamiento; sin miscast ni perfect invocation; dispersables normalmente; un modelo solo puede lanzar un Bound spell por fase; no convierte al modelo en Wizard.
**Forgefire (17)**: If this character joins a unit, that unit will gain the Armour Bane (2) and Flaming Attacks special rules. Should this character leave a unit it has joined for any reason, that unit loses these special rules.
**Slayer of Daemons (18)**: When this character makes a roll To Wound, a roll of 4+ is always a success, regardless of the target's Toughness. In addition, each unsaved wound inflicted by this character against an enemy model with the Warp-spawned special rule, or whose troop type is 'behemoth', has the Multiple Wounds (D3) special rule.
**Slayer of Dragons (18)**: When this character makes a roll To Wound, a roll of 4+ is always a success, regardless of the target's Toughness. In addition, each unsaved wound inflicted by this character against an enemy model whose troop type is 'behemoth' has the Multiple Wounds (D3) special rule.
**Artillery Master (19)**: Unless this character is fleeing or engaged in combat, once per turn, during the Shooting phase, a friendly unit of Quarrellers, unit of Thunderers or Dwarf war machine that is within its Command range can either re-roll any rolls To Hit of a natural 1, or re-roll a single Artillery dice.
**Entrenchment (19)**: During deployment, you may 'Entrench' a single non-character model whose troop type is 'war machine' for each character in your army with this special rule. An Entrenched war machine is considered to be behind partial cover and to be defending a low linear obstacle. Should the war machine move for any reason, it is no longer Entrenched.
**"Stand Back Chief" (19)**: A character with this special rule cannot be targeted by enemy shooting or by enemy spells whilst it is within 3" of a friendly unit whose troop type is 'war machine'.
**Venerable (21)**: Unless this unit is fleeing, friendly units within 6" of it can re-roll any failed Panic test.
**Royal Guard (24)**: Your army may include one unit of Hammerers for every King or Thane it includes. Any model in a unit of Hammerers that has been joined by a King or Thane can issue and accept challenges in the same manner as a character. Should the King or Thane leave the unit for any reason, the unit loses this ability.
**Stoic Defenders (24)**: During a turn in which it was charged by the enemy, a model with this special rule gains a +1 modifier to its Initiative and Attacks characteristics.
**Runes of Protection (25)**: This unit has a 6+ Ward save against any wounds suffered that were caused by a non-magical enemy attack.
**Runes of Warding (26)**: A model with this special rule has a 5+ Ward save against any wounds suffered that were caused by an attack that has the Flaming Attacks special rule. (No confundir con la runa talismánica "Rune of Warding".)
**"Fight Me!" (28)**: Any model with this special rule can issue and accept challenges in the same manner as a character.
**Slayer (28)**: When this model makes a roll To Wound, a roll of 4+ is always a success, regardless of the target's Toughness.
**Dive Bomb (29)**: Once per game, a unit with this special rule may perform a 'Dive Bomb' attack against a single enemy unit that is not engaged in combat. To do so, this unit must move (by flying) over the unit it wishes to attack during the Remaining Moves sub-phase. Once this unit's movement is complete, the enemy unit suffers D6 Strength 3 hits, each with an AP of -1, for each model in this unit that moved over it. However, for each roll of a natural 1 made when determining the number of hits, a bomb has misfired and this unit loses a single Wound instead.
**Bombing Run (30)**: This model may perform a 'Bombing Run' attack against a single enemy unit that is not engaged in combat. To do so, this model must move (by flying) over the unit it wishes to attack during the Remaining Moves sub-phase. Once this model's movement is complete, roll on the Bombing Run table (D6): **1 Premature Detonation**: This model loses a single Wound. **2 Dud**: The enemy unit loses a single Wound. **3-4 Direct Hit**: Place a large (5") blast template so that its central hole is directly over the centre of the enemy unit. Once placed, the template will scatter D6". Any model whose base lies underneath the template's final position risks being hit and suffering a single Strength 4 hit with an AP of -1. **5-6 Bombs Away**: Place two small (3") blast templates so that their central hole is over the enemy unit. Once placed, each template will scatter D6". Any model whose base lies underneath a template's final position risks being hit and suffering a single Strength 4 hit with an AP of -1. (Bombing Run **no** dice "once per game", a diferencia de Dive Bomb.)
**Shieldwall** [core p.178]: Once per game, during a turn in which it was charged, a unit with this special rule that is arrayed in a Close Order formation, and that is equipped with and chooses to use shields, may Give Ground rather than Fall Back in Good Order. (Dwarf Warriors, Longbeards y Hammerers solo tienen escudo si lo compran; Ironbreakers lo llevan de serie.)

## 8. Runas (PDF 33-39)
### 8.1 Sistema (PDF 33, texto exacto)
- Los modelos de un ejército Dwarfen Mountain Holds **no pueden comprar ningún objeto mágico del Core Rulebook**. En su lugar llevan runic items: "effectively magic items tailored to your own requirements by purchasing and combining the runes".
- **Cinco categorías**: Weapon, Armour, Talismanic, Standard, Engineering. Se elige(n) la(s) runa(s) y el objeto sobre el que se inscriben, se anotan en la lista y se suma el coste al modelo "just as you would for a magic item". Los límites en puntos los fija cada entrada (sección 3-6, resumen en 8.7).
- **The Rule Of Three**: No single item can have more than three runes. No model can have more than three runes from each category.
- **The Rule Of Form**: No item can have a rune not intended for that item. For example, a hand weapon cannot have an Engineering rune.
- **The Rule Of Pride**: No two runic items in your army can be identical. This applies to items with only a single rune and to items with two or three runes.
- **The Rule Of Jealousy**: Each Master rune may only be chosen once per army. No item can have more than one Master rune.
- **The Rule Of Duplication**: Only runes marked with an asterisk (*) can be duplicated on the same item, combining together and increasing their effect.
- "a runic item is no different in principle to any other magic item, and that all of the usual rules for magic items apply to runic items as well." [Core p.338-339: un modelo solo puede tener un objeto mágico por categoría; un arma mágica no se combina con arma adicional para ataque extra; no se puede dejar el arma mágica para usar una mundana.]
- **Single Use Runes**: Such runes can only be used once per game. Once used, the rune's magic is spent and it cannot be used again.
- Nota: en las runas el asterisco (*) significa **duplicable en el mismo objeto** (Rule of Duplication), no "extremely common" del Core.

## 10. NO ESTÁ EN LAS FUENTES / datos dudosos
1. **Armies of Infamy** de Dwarfen Mountain Holds: mencionadas en Allies (PDF 12) pero no existen en FoF ni en los demás archivos disponibles.
2. **Mercenaries (20%)**: el libro no lista unidades.
3. **Máximo de tamaño de unidad**: no impreso (solo "5+"), salvo Gyrocopters 1-6.
4. **Personajes con nombre**: ninguno. Tampoco hay monturas salvo Shieldbearers.
5. **Organ Gun y Flame Cannon** no tienen línea "Options": tal como está impreso **no pueden comprar Engineering runes** (Bolt Thrower, Grudge Thrower y Cannon sí, hasta 100).
6. **Quarrellers y Thunderers**: sin opciones de runas (ni Standard ni de campeón) ni de 0-1 especiales.
7. **Anvil of Doom**: "Base Size: N/A" (no se da tamaño de peana). "Immovable Object" está en su página pero no en su lista de Special Rules. El perfil del Anvil solo da T7 W5; el Forgefather & Anvil Guard (T5 W4) es un único perfil combinado; el libro no indica cómo se reparten las heridas entre Anvil y guardia (aplicar reglas de war machine del Core). No dice explícitamente si el Anvil puede ser General (es "War machine (character)", contado en el mismo 0-1/1.000 que King y Runelord).
8. **Master Rune of Balance / Rune of Spellbreaking**: "Anvil of Doom and Dwarf Runesmiths only". Interpretación: "Dwarf Runesmiths" es el nombre de la entrada (PDF 17) que incluye **Runelord y Runesmith**; no está dicho literalmente para el Runelord.
9. **Hammerers - great hammers y Weapon runes del Royal Champion**: las Weapon runes se inscriben en "hand weapon or great weapon (whatever form it takes)"; el libro no aclara si el great hammer cuenta como great weapon (ya tiene Magical Attacks). Las runas "Hand weapon only" (Flight, Cleaving, Speed) no van en él.
10. **Rune of Hesitation**: el título es "Master Rune of Hesitation" (45 pts) pero el texto del efecto dice "Rune of Hesitation". Se trata como Master rune (Rule of Jealousy).
11. **Rule of Three por categoría**: "No model can have more than three runes from each category" — un personaje con armas y armadura puede llegar a 3 Weapon + 3 Armour + 3 Talismanic (+3 Standard si BSB) dentro de su límite de puntos. Cómo interactúa con "un objeto mágico por categoría" del Core (que el libro dice que se aplica) no está aclarado: interpretación probable = un runic item por categoría por modelo (un arma rúnica, una armadura rúnica, un talismán rúnico).
12. **Rule of Pride y Standard runes de unidades**: no aclara excepciones; aplica a todo runic item (incluidos estandartes y máquinas): dos máquinas con la misma combinación de runas no son legales.
13. **Precios del Engineer** (pistol +6, handgun +8) difieren de King/Thane (pistol +5, handgun +6): tal cual impreso.
14. **Gyrocopter vs Gyrobomber**: troop types distintos impresos (Monstrous cavalry / Monstrous creature); ambos con un solo perfil; ninguno tiene Hatred, MR ni Resolute. Gyrobomber no tiene Skirmishers sino Close Order.
15. **Slayers (unidad)**: no hay campeón; los Giant Slayers son upgrade por modelo (+7). "Motley Crew*" lleva asterisco remitiendo a la nota impresa, no a la regla Motley Crew del Core (si existe, no se transcribe).
16. **Rune of Iron** no lleva asterisco (no duplicable); **Rune of Shielding** sí (Ward mejora 1 por runa adicional: 3 runas = 4+).
17. **AV (†)** de todas las filas: derivado de armadura+escudo según Core; el libro no imprime "Armour Value".
